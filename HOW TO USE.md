# Money Account — how to use

Your personal money book on your phone: spending, income, investments at today's prices, loans, insurance cover, tax relief receipts, reports for your accountant, and split bills with friends.

**Your records stay on your phone.** Nothing is saved on any server. Back up from More → Settings → Backup now and then (save the file to Google Drive or email it to yourself).

## Tabs
| Tab | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| 🏠 Home | Net worth, total assets and debts, this month's income/spending/savings, budgets, alerts (premiums due, FD maturing, people late paying you, backup reminder), health check (emergency fund, loan load, insurance cover) |
| 🧾 Records | Every expense, income and transfer by month. 🎙️ say it, 📷 scan receipt, 🔁 repeating records |
| 💎 Wealth | Investments (live prices), bank / e-wallet / card accounts, ⚖️ balance check, loans, money people owe you / you owe, investment reports |
| 🍻 Split | Outings with friends: who paid, who owes, fewest transfers to settle, copy for WhatsApp |
| ☰ More | Insurance, Tax relief, 📑 Reports & accounts, 🗂️ Receipts library, Budgets, Settings & backup |

The gold ＋ button adds anything: spending, income, voice note, receipt, statement scan, balance check, split bill, investment, loan, insurance.

## Recording fast
- **🎙️ Say it** — one long voice note for the whole day is fine, in rojak: *"Pagi tapau nasi lemak 8.50, minyak 80 pakai TNG, client lunch 45 business, gaji masuk Maybank 3k5, top up TNG 100 from Maybank, cash tinggal 230."* AI makes one record per amount (spending, income, transfers) and treats "tinggal / baki / 剩" as the account balance now, recording any gap as unrecorded spending. You tick and fix before saving.
- **📷 Scan receipt** — photo of a receipt, bill, payslip, commission statement or payment screenshot. AI fills in the amount, date, category, a tax relief if it fits, and whether it looks like an agency business expense. The photo stays with the record.
- **🔁 Repeat** — when adding a record, pick every month / 2 months / quarterly / half-yearly / yearly. Missed ones up to today are added on their own; the next ones are added when they fall due.
- **⚖️ Balance check** — forgot what you spent? Type what your bank / e-wallet / wallet really shows. The gap is recorded as "❓ Unrecorded spending" so your totals stay true. Re-label it later if you remember.

## Investments
- Shares (Bursa codes end in `.KL`, e.g. Maybank = `1155.KL`; US like `AAPL`), crypto and gold update to today's price by themselves. Foreign prices are converted to RM at today's rate.
- Unit trusts / ILP: tap **📷 Scan statement** on Wealth, take photos of your Public Mutual statement (or your handwritten list). AI reads each fund's units, price and cost, updates your holdings and writes a short report (saved under Investment reports). The report is for understanding, not advice.
- FD earns interest day by day until maturity. EPF and property: update the value when you get a statement.
- Buy more / Sell / Dividend on each holding keeps your average cost right.

## Tax and your accountant
- Tag receipts with a **tax relief** type and tick **💼 Business expense** for costs of earning agency income.
- More → **Tax relief** shows each relief against its limit (defaults are LHDN YA2025 limits — edit them when the new year's limits are announced) and a tax estimate.
- More → **Reports**:
  - **Dr / Cr statement** per account with running balance (Debit = money in, Credit = money out).
  - **Income statement** and **Assets & liabilities** for any period.
  - **Tax pack**: income by source (commission/override as business income, salary, dividends, rental), business expenses, reliefs, insurance premiums, estimate. Export as **PDF** (print → Save as PDF), **Excel (CSV)**, and a **Receipts pack** — one file with every receipt photo numbered R001, R002… matching the tax pack. Send them to your tax agent by email or WhatsApp.

## Split bills
Make an outing, add friends, add each bill: paid by one or several people, split equally, exact RM, shares, %, or **by items** (tick who had what; service charge and SST shared in proportion; type the receipt grand total to match it to the sen). Settle-up shows the fewest transfers; tap **Paid ✓** when money comes in. **Add my share to my records** puts only your part into your spending.

## Settings
Name, hide amounts (🙈 for showing the app to others), planning targets (yearly income, cover multiples, emergency months), server link and AI access code, money sound on/off, backup / restore, erase.
